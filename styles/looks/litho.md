Vintage lithograph look:
- 1930s-50s travel-poster lithography: bold simplified shapes, warm aged palette, subtle grain.
- Dramatic perspective or a heroic low angle on the subject.
- A thin period border rule framing the artwork.
- Any lettering is set in retro display type, ink-dark and generously sized on unprinted
  ground — period-authentic small tonal type goes muddy against the grain and aged palette.
